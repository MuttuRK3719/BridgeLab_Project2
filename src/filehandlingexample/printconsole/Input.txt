🌱 Meaning in a nutshell: It reminds us that while we’re caught up in planning for the future—chasing goals, setting timelines, dreaming big—real life is unfolding right now, often in unexpected ways. The everyday moments, the surprises, the setbacks, the joys—they're all part of life, whether we planned for them or not.
🧠 Deeper interpretation:
- It’s a call to be present. Don’t let your focus on what’s next blind you to what’s happening now.
- It hints at the unpredictability of life. No matter how carefully we plan, life has its own rhythm and surprises.
- Philosophically, it even touches on determinism—the idea that events unfold beyond our control, shaped by forces larger than our intentions.
